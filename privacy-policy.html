Privacy Policy for Quran Feed

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Quran Feed ("the app") is developed and published by PIXELSCLINIC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is handled, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version: you can use Quran Feed without giving us anything. Signing in is optional, we show no advertisements, and we never sell your information.

1. Using the app without an account

You do not need an account to read, listen, search, save favourites, or write notes. If you never sign in, everything you create stays on your device only. The following is stored locally on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Your reading preferences, such as theme, Arabic script, font sizes, and selected translation
  • Your reminder time and notification settings
  • Your place in the feed
  • Your favourites and notes, while you are signed out
  • Recitation audio files that you choose to download

Uninstalling the app removes all of this local data.

2. If you choose to sign in

Signing in with Google is optional. Its only purpose is to back up your favourites and notes so you do not lose them when you change or reset your phone. If you sign in, we handle:

  • Your Google account identifier and display name. These come from Google Sign In and are used to identify your backup and to greet you in the app. Your email address is held by Google Firebase Authentication as part of the sign in process.
  • Your favourites. A list of the ayah references you have saved.
  • Your notes. The text you write against an ayah. Please keep in mind that whatever you type in a note is stored in your backup, so avoid putting sensitive personal information there.

This information is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication). Access is restricted by security rules so that only your signed in account can read or write your own record. We do not browse, analyse, or share the contents of your notes.

3. Crash reports and usage measurement

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • Crashlytics sends us a report when the app crashes. A report contains the error details, your device model, and your operating system version. It helps us find and fix faults. It does not contain your notes.
  • Analytics records aggregate usage such as app opens and session length, so we can see which parts of the app are actually used. We look at this in aggregate, not per person.

4. Recitation audio

Recitation audio is not bundled with the app. When you play or download a recitation, the file is requested from mirrors.quranicaudio.com. As with any internet request, that server can see your IP address. We do not control that service and receive no information from it.

5. Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled and displayed entirely on your device. No reminder information is sent to us or to any server.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements and we include no advertising or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone
  • We do not ask for your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you

7. Permissions the app requests

  • Internet. Required to fetch recitation audio and to sync your backup when you are signed in.
  • Notifications. Required only if you switch on daily reminders.
  • Run at startup. Required only so that your scheduled reminders survive a device restart.

8. How long information is kept

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you are signed in, your favourites and notes are kept in your backup until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.

9. Deleting your data

You are in control of your information:

  • Delete individual items. Remove a favourite or a note in the app and it is removed from your backup too.
  • Stop syncing. Sign out at any time from Settings. Your device keeps working offline.
  • Delete everything. Email us at pixelsclinic@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for account deletion. We will delete your authentication record and your stored favourites and notes within 30 days and confirm once it is done.

10. Children

Quran Feed is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in and provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where information is processed

Google Firebase processes and stores information on Google's servers, which may be located outside your country. Google's own handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

12. Security

Traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit. Access to your backup is restricted by Firestore security rules to your own signed in account. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we limit what we collect specifically so that there is very little to expose.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
